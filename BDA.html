Blood Donation Awareness

Donate blood and save lives. One donation can save up to 3 people.

1. Why Donate Blood?

Blood is needed for accidents, surgeries, and serious patients. Blood cannot be manufactured, only humans can donate it.

2. Eligibility Criteria

  • Age: 18 to 65 years
  • Weight: Minimum 50 kg
  • Hemoglobin: Minimum 12.5 g/dl
  • No fever, cold, or recent tattoo in last 6 months

3. Donation Process

  1. Registration
  2. Medical Checkup
  3. Blood Donation (takes 10 minutes)
  4. Refreshment and Rest
